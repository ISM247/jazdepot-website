Good to know

Common questions

Do you repair reefer units other than Carrier?

Yes. We're an Authorized Carrier Transicold Dealer and service reefer units and generator sets of all brands at our Norfolk yard.

Do you repair dry containers to IICL standard?

Yes, and we carry out condition surveys.

What do drivers need before coming to the yard?

A pre-advice of the acceptance or release. Gate hours are Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM–4:00 PM.

Do you deliver?

Yes: tilt bed, flatbed, drayage and intermodal across Hampton Roads and beyond.

What fabrication do you do?

Roll-up doors, doors, windows, shelving, lighting and outlets, painting, and custom work.
